WHY GQ SOUNDS

Talent is everywhere.
Discovery shouldn’t be.

GQ Sounds is an independent home for music made in and around Gqeberha. We put emerging artists where curious listeners can find them.

Artists keep their rights. We bring the spotlight, not a contract with tiny print.
